# Electron Scattering Angle Analysis

**Analysis**: Elastic scattering (ES) of supernova neutrinos

## Dataset Overview

- **Categories analyzed**: 50
- **Total events**: 336,565

## Key Findings

- **Median scattering angle**: 7.87°
- **Mean scattering angle**: 10.07°
- **Correlation with E_ν**: -0.2781
- **Electrons carry**: ~71% of neutrino energy

## Physics Interpretation

The small scattering angles (~8°) represent the **theoretical limit** for supernova neutrino pointing using ES interactions. Higher energy neutrinos produce more forward-peaked scattering.
